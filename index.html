About Me

I am a PhD Candidate at Sun Yat-sen University (SYSU), supervised by Prof. Heye Zhang, and currently a Visiting PhD Student in the Department of Bioengineering at Imperial College London, supervised by Prof. Guang Yang. My research sits at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and cardiovascular medicine. I develop physics-informed neural networks and operator learning methods for non-invasive coronary hemodynamic assessment (FFR, IMR), and intelligent reinforcement learning systems for virtual coronary intervention planning — with the goal of improving clinical decision-making in the catheterisation laboratory.

Research Experience

AI-guided Virtual PCI Planning and Stent Optimization
Sun Yat-sen University / Imperial College London · 2024 — Present
Developing an intelligent interventional planning framework that moves beyond functional diagnosis toward treatment strategy optimization. This work integrates coronary image reconstruction, FFR curve prediction, fast hemodynamic surrogate modeling, and physiology-aware decision learning to evaluate counterfactual stent deployment strategies, including stent position, length, and diameter. The long-term goal is to build an end-to-end virtual PCI system that supports interpretable, verifiable, and interactive personalized coronary intervention planning.
Outcome: IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging (2026)
Physics-informed Neural Operators for Cardiovascular Digital Physiology
Sun Yat-sen University · 2023 — Present
Designed physics-informed and physics-encoded learning frameworks that map vascular anatomy and physiological boundary conditions to continuous pressure–flow fields. Rather than treating FFR prediction as a black-box regression problem, these methods incorporate geometric associations, Navier–Stokes-based physical constraints, local variational residuals, tree-structured vascular topology, and test-time domain adaptation into the learning process. This research enables continuous FFR curve prediction, improved cross-modality generalization, and interpretable hemodynamic modeling across cardiovascular systems.
Outcome: IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging, 2024; Medical Image Analysis, 2025; Expert Systems with Applications, 2026.
Image-derived Cardiovascular Hemodynamics
Sun Yat-sen University · 2022 — 2024
Developed patient-specific computational models for recovering clinically relevant pressure–flow biomarkers from routine cardiovascular imaging. This line of work focuses on physiological boundary-condition modeling, side-branch flow compensation, coronary pressure-field computation, and CTA/angiography-derived functional assessment. These methods enable pressure-wire-free FFR estimation and extend anatomical imaging toward quantitative digital physiology.
Outcome: IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, 2024; Computers in Biology and Medicine, 2024.
